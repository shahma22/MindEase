💡 Why breathing exercises help

Controlled breathing activates your parasympathetic nervous system — the part responsible for rest and calm. It lowers cortisol (the stress hormone), reduces heart rate, and signals to your brain that you are safe. Even 2 minutes of guided breathing can noticeably reduce anxiety.
