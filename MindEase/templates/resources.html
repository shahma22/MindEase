💡 Wellness Resources

Evidence-based techniques to help you manage stress and support your mental wellbeing.

🌬️ Breathing Exercise (4-7-8)

  • Inhale through your nose for 4 seconds
  • Hold your breath for 7 seconds
  • Exhale slowly for 8 seconds
  • Repeat 3–4 times

🌳 Grounding Technique (5-4-3-2-1)

  • Name 5 things you can see
  • Name 4 things you can touch
  • Name 3 things you can hear
  • Name 2 things you can smell
  • Name 1 thing you can taste

📚 Managing Academic Stress

  • Break large tasks into smaller daily steps
  • Use Pomodoro: 25 min work, 5 min break
  • Set one achievable goal per day
  • Talk to your personal tutor or student support
  • Avoid all-night study sessions

🏃 Physical Wellbeing

  • A 10-minute walk reduces anxiety significantly
  • Aim for 7–9 hours of sleep each night
  • Reduce caffeine after 2pm
  • Drink water consistently throughout the day
  • Stretch after long study sessions

💬 Positive Self-Talk

  • "I am doing my best with what I have."
  • "This feeling is temporary."
  • "Progress matters more than perfection."
  • "It is okay to ask for help."
  • "I have handled difficult things before."
